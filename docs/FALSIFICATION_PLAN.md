# Falsification plan

The project must preserve informative failure.

PCC is weakened in this domain if:

1. observable histories cannot recover known simulated mixtures above ordinary
   action-frequency baselines;
2. recovered dimensions are unstable across seeds or policy implementations;
3. PCC features add no held-out prediction beyond equity, pot, position, and
   betting history;
4. PCC-constrained dynamics fit worse than generic HMM, VAR, or sequence models;
5. proposed counter-relations disappear after controlling for information
   state, stack/pot structure, and seat;
6. human-data results depend on labeling entire players rather than decisions;
7. Chaos reduces to residual error or ineffective random action;
8. Control reduces to hand strength or ordinary tightness;
9. Pressure reduces to raise frequency.

The proposed cycle is an empirical outcome, never a coded payoff modifier.

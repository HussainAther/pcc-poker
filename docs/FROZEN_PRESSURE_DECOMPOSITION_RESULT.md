# Frozen Pressure Mechanism Decomposition Result

## Status

Completed prospective experiment.

Control policy was frozen and unchanged.

## Primary estimand

Loss of Control contextual-alignment advantage after removal
of one engineered Pressure mechanism.

## Results

| Pressure variant | Alignment effect | 95% interval |
|---|---:|---:|
| Full | 0.2290 | [0.1571, 0.3009] |
| No fold leverage | 0.1871 | [0.1194, 0.2547] |
| No strength selectivity | -0.0231 | [-0.0397, -0.0066] |

## Attenuation

- Remove fold leverage: 18.3%
- Remove strength selectivity: 110.1%

Prespecified qualification threshold: 50%.

Only removal of strength selectivity qualified.

## Interpretation

Within the engineered Adaptive Pressure policy family,
Control's previously observed contextual-alignment advantage
depends strongly on strength-selective Pressure.

Removing learned fold leverage does not materially attenuate
the effect under the prespecified criterion.

Removing strength selectivity eliminates the positive Control
alignment advantage and produces a small negative mean effect.

This supports a mechanistic role for strength-selective
Pressure in this engineered system.

It does not establish that human poker behavior, biological
systems, or other domains instantiate the same mechanism.

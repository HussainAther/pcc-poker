# PCC measurement contract for poker

## Unit of analysis

The unit is a decision at an information state—not a player, hand, or action
name. Mode weights may vary over time.

## Pressure

Immediate coercive leverage: initiative, commitment, fold leverage, and
response-space compression. Pressure is evaluated before observing the
opponent's response.

## Control

Prediction-weighted value across an opponent range and response distribution,
minus action risk. Control is not passive play and is not synonymous with
calling or checking.

## Chaos

Conditional action surprise relative to the actor's context-conditioned action
history (and, later, an out-of-sample opponent predictor),
subject to an expected-value floor. Strategic effectiveness is reported
separately from surprise.

## Required comparisons

- cards/equity, pot, position, and betting history;
- conventional aggression and action-frequency statistics;
- equilibrium or approximate-equilibrium action probabilities when available;
- generic latent-state and sequence models;
- PCC-constrained alternatives.

## Valid evidence

PCC receives support only from held-out identifiability, incremental prediction,
dynamical fit, conditional counter-relations, and transfer. Naming three model
dimensions Pressure, Control, and Chaos is not evidence.
